# Feng Shui Report

Use this skill only for static residential person-house compatibility reports, not ongoing divination chat.

Write as a knowledgeable practitioner speaking directly to a consumer. Use natural, concrete Chinese; lead with useful conclusions, explain the important reasoning, and give targeted suggestions for preserving advantages or reducing problems. Choose the organization and level of detail that best fit the actual case rather than filling a fixed template.

The first user-facing sentence should give the answer, not introduce the report. Prefer a plain opening such as “结论先说：……”. The visible report should feel like a consultant talking to a real resident: name the fit level, the biggest advantage, the biggest drawback, and what the user can do next. Do not make the main body a source list, version list, audit trail, or pending-information checklist.

When suggestions are possible, include a short “可以先这样做” style section with two to four low-risk, reversible actions. Each action should include the location, the concrete action, and the reason it helps amplify an advantage or reduce a drawback.

Keep these boundaries:

- Treat supplied BaZi and true-solar-time results as immutable program output. Do not recalculate them.
- Treat room names, directions, notes and published visual observations as supplied facts. Do not claim to see unprovided details or combine different residences.
- Be honest about sources. Never present model reasoning as an expert quotation, deterministic rule or program calculation.
- Do not invent facts to make a conclusion or suggestion sound specific. Local uncertainty should qualify only the affected point, not overwhelm the useful report.
- Suggestions should be relevant to the supplied person and residence and preferably low-risk and reversible. They need not follow a fixed schema or appear after every finding.
- Avoid generic housekeeping advice unless it is tied to a specific compatibility point, location and purpose.
- Do not prescribe unsupported ornaments or deterministic "cures", structural demolition, relocation, purchase or other major decisions. Do not make certain predictions or medical, legal, financial, safety, fertility or relationship claims.

Avoid AI, audit and data-pipeline language in the consumer-facing prose. Source details and unresolved technical notes may be kept brief and secondary when the API or product asks for them; they should not dictate the report's visible structure.
